My Cybersecurity Awareness

This page connects what we're learning in my Computer Security course to how I think about technology and online safety. Cybersecurity is vital because so much of our personal information, schoolwork, communication, and everyday activity depends on computers and the internet.

Course Connection

Building My Cybersecurity Knowledge

As I continue learning about cybersecurity, I want to better understand how computers and networks are protected from different types of threats. I’m especially interested in topics like network security, malware, passwords, phishing, and how security problems can be found and prevented.

The Core Pillars: The CIA Triad

C Confidentiality: Ensuring sensitive information is accessible only to authorized entities and kept protected from unauthorized interception.
I Integrity: Safeguarding the accuracy, completeness, and tamper-resistance of data and software throughout its lifecycle.
A Availability: Guaranteeing authorized users timely and uninterrupted access to critical networks, systems, and services.

Cybersecurity Questions & In-Depth Answers

Articulated perspectives addressing the four required course prompts:

Question 01

What does cybersecurity mean to you?

To me, cybersecurity means keeping computers, networks, accounts, and personal information safe from people who may try to access or misuse them.

Information Security Network Safety Account Protection Data Privacy
Question 02

Why is cybersecurity important?

Cybersecurity is important because so much of our personal and work-related information is stored online. It helps protect people and businesses from hacked accounts, stolen information, scams, malware, and other online threats.

Online Protection Data Security Threat Prevention Anti-Scam & Malware
Question 03

What is one cybersecurity habit you currently practice?

One cybersecurity habit I practice is using strong passwords and being careful with suspicious links or emails. I try not to click on anything that looks unusual or asks for personal information.

Strong Passwords Phishing Awareness Email Security Safe Browsing
Question 04

What cybersecurity topic do you want to learn this semester?

This semester, I would like to learn more about how computer systems are protected from attacks and how security problems are found and fixed. I’m especially interested in learning more about network security, malware, and the different ways attackers try to gain access to systems.

System Protection Network Security Malware Analysis Vulnerability Fixing

Cybersecurity Project Proposal

Project Focus: Phishing Awareness

For my cybersecurity project, I plan to create a web application that helps users learn how to recognize phishing attempts. The website would be designed for students and everyday internet users who may come across suspicious emails, messages, or links.

The application would include examples of phishing and legitimate messages, short quizzes, explanations of warning signs, and tips for staying safe online. It would also avoid collecting any real passwords or sensitive personal information.

👥 Target Users

The website would be designed for students and everyday internet users who want to improve their ability to recognize phishing attempts and stay safer online.

📋 Functional Requirements

The application would include examples of phishing and legitimate messages, short quizzes, explanations of common warning signs, safety tips, and a score that shows how well the user did.

⚠️ Potential Threats

Phishing emails, fake login pages, malicious links, social engineering, and stolen account information.

🔍 Research Question

How can an interactive web application help users better recognize and avoid phishing attacks?

🎯 Project Objective

The goal of the project is to improve phishing awareness and help users become more confident when identifying suspicious messages and online scams.
