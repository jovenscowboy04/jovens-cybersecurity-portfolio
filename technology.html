My Relationship with Technology

Technology has always been something I’ve been interested in, especially computers and gaming. Over time, I became more curious about how computers work, how software is built, and how different systems connect with each other.

Today, I use technology every day for school, gaming, programming, and learning new skills. I’m especially interested in cybersecurity, networking, web development, and computer hardware. I also enjoy learning about newer areas like artificial intelligence. As I continue studying IT, I want to keep improving my skills and better understand how technology can be used and protected.
